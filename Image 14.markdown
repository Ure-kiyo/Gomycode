## Brand & Style
The design system balances the clinical precision of conversational artificial intelligence with the warm, tactile curation of high-end boutique shopping. The interface evokes effortless clarity, discernment, and trust—cutting through commercial noise to deliver immediate, verified product intelligence.

The visual direction combines Modern Minimalism with subtle Glassmorphic and Tactile layers: generous negative space, soft ambient illumination, and sculpted card surfaces. The user should never feel pressured by aggressive commercial patterns; rather, interactions feel like consulting an articulate, impeccably informed personal shopper. Micro-surfaces feature precise 1px borders with delicate atmospheric depth, ensuring every recommendation feels bespoke, vetted, and premium.

## Colors
The palette balances deep intellectual indigo tones against dynamic, energetic warm accents to structure conversation and commerce seamlessly.
